#### Seasonally-adjusted Quarterly Total Factor Productivity in Canada

The seasonally-adjusted quarterly total factor productivity (TFP) in Canadian business sector is built by extending the method of measuring annual aggregate productivity by [Diewert and Yu (2012)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/sls/ipmsls/v24y20123.html). The estimated quarterly TFP is not adjusted for capacity utilization.

Data file: [tfpQtr1976_2018Web.ods](/files/tfpQtr1976_2018Web.ods)

Notes on the data: [QuarterlyTFP20210420.pdf](/files/QuarterlyTFP20210420.pdf)


Previously, [Sharon Kozicki](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/profile/sharon-kozicki/) and I estimated the seasonally unadjusted series of quarterly TFP, these data are not maintained and not updated. Link to the [paper](http://www.bankofcanada.ca/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/wp2015-6.pdf).
