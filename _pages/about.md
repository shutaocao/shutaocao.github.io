I am an associate professor in economics at Trent University, a beautiful campus located on the Otonabee river. Before joining Trent, I worked at the Bank of Canada and Victoria University of Wellington. My research interest is macroeconomics, with a focus on distributional aspects of policy and aggregate implications of heterogeneity.
